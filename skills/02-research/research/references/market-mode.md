# Market Research

Understand real user problems, alternatives and purchase behavior. Use [report-spine.md](report-spine.md) for presentation.

## Select the question

- Problem discovery: who experiences the problem, when and how they currently cope.
- Competitors: which alternatives solve the same job, for whom and under what constraints.
- Market sizing: obtainable users and budgets, with explicit assumptions.
- Distribution: which reachable audience and channel can test demand.

Do not produce all four studies for a narrow question.

## Collect real evidence

Use actual interviews, product and pricing pages, public reports, reviews and accessible communities. Chinese sources may include V2EX, LINUX DO, Douban and Xiaohongshu when relevant and available.
Quote only retrieved material, preserve source and context, and do not fabricate users, interviews, payment intent or inaccessible content.
Distinguish past behavior and actual spending from stated preferences. Report sample selection and channel bias; public posts are not representative surveys by default.

## Compare and estimate

Compare alternatives on the same user job and constraints, including manual workarounds and doing nothing.
For market estimates state the population, accessible segment, adoption assumption and revenue basis. Use bottom-up checks and ranges where justified, rather than presenting broad market totals as obtainable demand.
Separate measured facts, estimates and unknowns. Verify current prices and licensing from the primary source.

## Recommend an experiment

Propose a bounded test of the main uncertainty: audience, offer, channel, expected evidence, cost and stop condition. Do not treat interest, stars or traffic as proof of purchase.

## Verify

- User evidence is traceable and its selection limits are visible.
- Competitors share a comparison basis; claims and prices are sourced.
- Sizing assumptions and uncertainty are explicit.
- The conclusion states what is supported and what demand evidence is still needed.
