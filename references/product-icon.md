# Product Icon Design

Design an ownable product mark, favicon or app icon. Distinguish it from a UI control glyph; simplicity alone does not make either unsuitable.

## Direction

Follow the brand brief. For restrained developer-tool branding, the preferred direction is:

- Near-black backing (`#0c0c0f` or `#16161a`) with a precise indigo accent (`#5b5bd6`), or white backing with dark strokes.
- Deliberate geometric cuts, industrial lines and negative space; a clear silhouette and optical balance.
- Linework or flat geometry rather than gradient fills, glowing cores or neural-network motifs.
- Complexity determined by recognition and meaning, not a minimum shape or color count. A considered simple mark can be complete.

These are visual preferences for the corresponding brief, not restrictions on every brand category.

## Reference method

Study mature brands for geometry, rhythm, spacing and reduction. Extract principles rather than copying their marks.

| Source | Study |
|---|---|
| [Vercel brand](https://vercel.com/geist/brands) | Symbol reduction, clear space and small-size use |
| [Linear brand](https://linear.app/brand) | Geometric construction and controlled identity |
| [Datadog resources](https://www.datadoghq.com/about/resources/) | Distinctive silhouette and recognizable character |

Use real observed assets. Brand references are not necessarily open-source, and software licenses do not confer trademark rights.
Avoid describing another brand's exact geometry from memory; inspect its official assets first.
For broader visual retrieval use [design-research](../skills/03-design/design-research/SKILL.md).

## Execute and verify

1. Define the product meaning and one visual idea; sketch distinct directions only as needed.
2. Refine silhouette, stroke weight, corners, counters and negative space; remove arbitrary ornament.
3. Check at intended small sizes, in monochrome, and on dark and light backgrounds. Optically align instead of relying only on mathematical centering.
4. Deliver only agreed variants and paths. Use editable SVG for code-native geometry and image generation for raster concepts when appropriate.

Evidence: rendered small-size comparison, monochrome result and explanation of how the mark relates to the product.
Do not claim quality from gradients, dimensionality or shape count alone.
