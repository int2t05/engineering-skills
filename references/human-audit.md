# Human Audit

Documents support human decisions. A valid draft is ready for review, not human-approved.

## Agree the artifact

Confirm purpose, reader and path before creating, moving or renaming a document.
An explicit user-supplied path or approved output map is authorization; do not ask again unless it changes.
Skill paths are suggestions. Reuse existing owners of the subject and generate only documents with a durable consumer: a decision, contract, operation or learning.
Small edits, routine test runs and simple explanations usually fit in the conversation. Do not enforce a fixed document count or delete useful evidence to meet it.

## Make review easy

- First screen: purpose, scope, status and decisions the reader must make.
- Separate confirmed requirements, source facts, proposals and unverified claims.
- State rules once. Use stable IDs when readers must trace requirements or findings.
- Short paragraphs, readable tables and explained terminology; language follows the reader.
- Mermaid explains relationships, sequences and states. No figure quotas or decorative diagrams.
- Evidence identifies the source, code or measurement, its conditions and limitations.
- Keep necessary technical versions and honest incomplete states; editing history belongs in Git or audit reports.

## Audit by risk

Each item names a question, evidence, acceptance condition and status. Choose relevant items, without a fixed count.
Reports use finding, evidence, recommendation and human decision. Record rejected findings and rationale in the report, rather than in operational instructions.
A simple explanation does not need a numbering system or an audit table.

## Approval and completion

- Draft: author work is incomplete.
- Ready for human review: author checks passed; unresolved decisions are visible.
- Confirmed: the human accepted the relevant scope and decisions.
- Verified in use: implementation or operation produced the stated evidence.

Human acceptance and technical verification are different. Never fabricate approval.
Complete authoring when the document is ready for review; report the pending human gate honestly.
Before dependent implementation, confirm governing requirements or designs when that gate was agreed. Continue independent authorized work.

## Task-specific writing

Tutorials explain one concept per small file; topic folders follow actual subject boundaries. Pair runnable examples with expected outcomes.
Exercises cite real official or upstream practice, compare against a known result or baseline, and explain the success criteria. Label adaptations; never invent provenance.
Research leads with usefulness and applicability, then evidence, relationships, mechanisms, limits and comparison. Separate facts from inference.
Blogs develop one argument with explained terms, supporting examples and verifiable sources.
Source wikis trace actual entry points and meaningful calls; distinguish observed behavior from intent. Never invent symbols, pronunciation or verified results.
Diagram and table density follows the subject. The detailed reusable prompts live in docs/workflow-prompts.md in the source pack.
